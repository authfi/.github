# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you have found a weakness in AuthFI, tell us first, in private.

**Email:** cybersecurity@authfi.io

Please do not open a public issue or discussion for a security report.

Include:

- what you found, and where (the address or the request);
- the steps to repeat it;
- what you expected, and what happened.

We ask that you give us time to fix a problem before you publish it, that you do not access data that is not yours, and that you do not test in a way that degrades the service for others.

Our contact details for security researchers are also published at <https://authfi.io/.well-known/security.txt>.
